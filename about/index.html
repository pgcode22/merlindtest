Corporate Profile & Formulation Philosophy

Veterinary Science Dedicated to Flock Resilience & Farm Productivity.

Founded with an uncompromising commitment to clinical rigor and agricultural biotech excellence, Merlin Life Care develops high-efficacy veterinary pharmaceuticals, synergistic acidifiers, and specialized nutritional compounds engineered specifically for commercial poultry producers across Pakistan.

Clinical Standards

Bridging Pharmaceutical Science with Real Commercial Farm Economics

Modern commercial poultry production faces unprecedented microbiological and environmental challenges. Extreme summer thermal stress, rising feed input costs, immunosuppressive viral pressures, and strict global standards on antimicrobial stewardship require smarter, cleaner therapeutics.

At Merlin Life Care, every batch is compounded with certified United States Pharmacopeia (USP) grade actives. We eliminate filler compounds, focusing entirely on maximum biological bioavailability, rapid water solubility, and measurable flock return on investment (ROI).
