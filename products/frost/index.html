Pharmaceutical Profile

Frost

Heat Stress & Cellular Osmoregulation Electrolyte Solution

Frost is an advanced anti-stress, osmoregulatory formula containing calibrated electrolytes, betaine, and high-dose Vitamin C to combat extreme thermal stress, prevent respiratory alkalosis, and stabilize flock hydration during peak heat cycles.

Active Formulation Composition

Pharmacopoeia Grade (USP)
Dextrose (USP)25,000 mg
Vitamin C (USP)50,000 mg
Glycerin (USP)20,000 mg
Potassium Chloride (USP)23,500 mg
Magnesium Chloride (USP)19,000 mg
Vitamin E (USP)1,000 mg
Betaine (USP)1,000 mg

Biological Actions & Indications

  • ✓ Combats Extreme Heat Stress: Re-establishes cellular electrolyte and ionic balance during intense panting, hyperventilation, and high ambient barn temperatures.
  • ✓ Blood pH Homeostasis: Rapidly neutralizes respiratory alkalosis resulting from excessive carbon dioxide (CO2) expiration during open-beak panting.
  • ✓ Cellular Osmoprotection: Natural Betaine maintains intracellular osmotic pressure, safeguarding intestinal enterocyte integrity and reducing dehydration mortality.
  • ✓ Immune & Free-Radical Protection: Mega-dose Vitamin C and Vitamin E scavenge damaging reactive oxygen species, halting heat-induced immunosuppression.

Administration & Dosage

Drinking Water Protocol

1 ml per 4 Liters of Drinking Water

Administer in morning drinking water during heat waves, high ambient temperature periods, or prior to flock transport.
